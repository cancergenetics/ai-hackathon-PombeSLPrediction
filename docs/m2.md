## What M2 is
- **Training pairs.** Per outer fold: `data.fold_split` → **`data.drop_noncoding`** (new, additive,
  tested). DESIGN §1 drops pseudogenes and ncRNAs. 11 non-protein-coding dev genes are removed, 4.6k–6.1k
  pairs per fold (table below). M1 already dropped them internally; B1/B4 (`run_baselines.py`, unchanged)
  still train on them.
- **Feature families** (each family is one ablation unit; `src/models/m2.family_of`):

  | family | content | count |
  |---|---|---|
  | LF | label-free pair features, `src/pairs.assemble`, identical to M1 (incl. HYPO) | 358 |
  | RYAN | RYAN-fold from `src/ryanfeat`: `seen_min/max`, B1 `b1_logit`, `beta_min/max`, MF score, training-profile correlation, gene mean −S | 9 |
  | NBR | `src/ryanfeat` NBR: 9 similarity sources + cross-source mean × 10 features (directional, block, support, neighbour rate) | 100 |
  | GP | gene-propensity model (new; below) | 8 |

- **Gene-propensity (GP) model** (`src/models/m2.py`). It is Ryan-derived, so it is nested exactly like RYAN/NBR. Within any training pair set T:
  1. B1 β for every training gene: the joint L2 additive logit fitted on T, the same fit as the RYAN `beta`.
  2. Gene-level inputs:
     - the 155 label-free gene-table columns of the feature store;
     - per similarity source, a **leave-own-pairs-out NBR gene rate**: the similarity-weighted mean
       training hit rate of the gene's top-k training neighbours, where each neighbour's rate excludes
       its pair with the gene itself;
     - the cross-source mean of those rates.
  3. Regressor β ~ inputs: the **mean of a LightGBM regressor** (300 rounds, 15 leaves, lr 0.03) **and
     a RidgeCV**. RidgeCV runs on inputs that are median-imputed, standardised and given missing
     indicators, all using the fitting genes only.
     - It is cross-fitted over 5 random folds of the training genes. Each training gene's β̂ comes from
       the one model that did not see it (out-of-fold).
     - Every other gene gets the mean of the 5 models.
  4. Pair features:
     - `gp_hat_{min,max,sum}` (β̂);
     - `gp_beta_{min,max}`: actual β of seen genes, NaN if unseen; min is NaN if either gene is unseen;
     - `gp_best_{min,max,sum}`: β if seen, else β̂.
  - Choice of regressor, made before any outer-fold run:
    - On real fold-0 training genes (internal OOF only, no eval labels), all candidates were within
      0.015 r: LightGBM 0.584, ridge 0.579, mean 0.593, more-regularised LightGBM 0.590.
    - On the toy data (truth known), ridge tracked the planted propensity of unseen genes much better
      than LightGBM (Spearman 0.60 vs 0.48); the mean scored 0.56.
    - The mean was chosen: best real OOF r, robust on toy. See "Toy check" for the pre-stated criterion
      this misses.
- **Nested stacking** (`src/stacking.inner_rows/outer_rows` with `builder=M2Builder`; `m2.fold_tables_m2`):
  - Stacker training rows are the inner eval rows of `data.inner_splits` on the drop_noncoding training
    pairs: C1-, C2- and C3-like, 5 inner folds, about 576k rows per outer fold.
  - Their RYAN/NBR/GP features come from that inner fold's training pairs only.
  - Outer eval rows get the same features from all outer-training pairs.
  - Cached per (split, fold, inner) via `src.data.cache_save/cache_load`. The cache name is
    `m2feat_<hash>`, which covers the feature-set version, the ryanfeat config, the similarity sha256s,
    the GP config and the gene-table sha256s. Ablations reuse the cache.
- **Stacker.**
  - Binary LightGBM with the M1 default config. The ≤6-config check below kept `default`.
  - Rows are weighted by protocol class weights per inner fold (`w` from src/stacking), rescaled to
    mean 1.
  - Model seed s: early stopping (100 rounds, max 2000) on the class-weighted AP of inner fold
    j = s mod 5. The model is fitted on the gene-grouped rows for j (`data.gene_grouped_validation`: no
    row touching a T_j gene, no validation pair). The 3 seeds therefore use 3 different inner
    validation folds.
  - Seeds 0, 1 and 2 are averaged in `evaluate()`.
  - `M2_init` adds the inner-fold B1 logit as `init_score`; the outer B1 logit is added at prediction.
  - Symmetry, checked every fold:
    - LF features of the swapped eval pairs are bit-identical;
    - predictions on 20k swapped rows are bit-identical;
    - RYAN, NBR and GP features are symmetric by construction (tested in `tests/test_m2.py`).
- **Ablations** use the same config and cache, one family dropped at a time:
  - `M1N` = LF + NBR, i.e. M2 minus RYAN-fold (β, MF, profile, seen flags) and minus GP, which is β-derived;
  - `M2_minus_GP`;
  - `M2_minus_NBR`;
  - `M2_init` (with init_score).

## Issues / notes for the PI
1. **Toy pre-stated criterion missed (reported, not changed).**
   - `scripts/verify_m2_toy.py` stated in advance: unseen-gene Spearman(β̂, true β) ≥ 0.6 for the full GP.
   - The chosen GP gives **0.563 (FAIL)**. INFB-only gives 0.659 (pass). NOISE-only gives 0.019 (pass).
     `gp_hat_sum` C3 AP is 0.149 = 8× prevalence (pass).
   - Diagnosis: the regression target is noisy. B1 β itself has Spearman only 0.55 with the true β on
     toy training genes, and the extra uninformative inputs (NOISE, MODF, NBR rates) cost ~0.1 ρ against
     INFB alone. The 0.6 bar was set without knowing the target noise.
   - Ridge alone reached 0.601 on toy but did no better on real OOF r. The mean ensemble was chosen on
     real OOF r *before* any outer run.
   - `tests/test_m2.py::test_toy_gp_tracks_true_propensity` is a regression guard with looser bounds
     (full > 0.4, NOISE |ρ| < 0.2, full > NOISE + 0.3), set after this check; it is labelled as such.
2. **Train-vs-eval shift of GP features on C2/C3.**
   - Stacker rows have mean `gp_hat_sum` 0.02 vs 0.14 on eval rows (KS 0.06–0.10).
   - A fold-0 diagnostic (scratch script, not committed) shows gene-level β̂ is calibrated:
     - mean β̂ of held-out *universe* genes: inner fold 0 = 0.047, outer = 0.051;
     - mean β of training genes: −0.002 in both;
     - intercept: −5.31 vs −5.36.
   - The pair-level difference therefore comes from pair composition. Stacker C2/C3 rows contain all
     training pairs of the held-out genes, including non-universe/DAmP partners and non-eval-eligible
     pairs; eval rows contain only eval-eligible universe pairs.
   - Two expected nested-stacking shifts:
     - NBR support is lower on stacker rows (C1 0.487 vs 0.535, KS 0.25), because inner training sets
       hold only ~58% of the outer pairs (neither gene in T_j, minus H_j).
     - On C3, `b1_logit`/`mf_score` are fold constants (intercepts) that differ between inner and outer
       fits (KS ≈ 1). Within-class ranking is unaffected, but they shift C3 against C1/C2 in the pooled
       ranking.
   - Possible follow-up: centre or rank-normalise β̂ and the intercept-like features per training set.
3. **B1/B4 still train on the 11 non-coding genes.** `run_baselines.py` was not changed, so its
   reruns reproduce the earlier numbers exactly. Only M1 and M2 drop the genes. The effect on B1/B4 is
   ≤ 1% of training pairs.
4. **Reproducibility of earlier dirty runs.** At the clean commit, B1 (0.1033), B4 (0.1079) and M1
   (0.1447) reproduce the dirty-tree numbers to 4 decimals. M1 runtime fell from 1,219 s to 352 s
   because the machine was much less loaded.
5. **Not done.**

   - Only one 6-config check, on fold 0 / inner fold 0. `lr0.02` was +0.0012, below the pre-set 0.005
     bar.
   - The 3 seeds use 3 different inner validation folds (bagging-like). More seeds might help, as
