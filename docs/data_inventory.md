**Scale (PI decision).** S2 (`s_score`) is the authors' pseudo-averaged score (Collins et al. 2006): for pairs measured in both orientations it is ~ the mean of the two raw orientation scores; for pairs measured in only one orientation the raw score is shrunk by a global monotone map, because a single measurement is noisier (verified exactly in `scripts/verify_core_data.py`). Equivalent thresholds: one orientation (1,275,030 pairs): S2 = -2.3 <-> raw -3.76; both orientations (62,003 pairs): S2 = -2.3 <-> raw -2.25. Applying -2.3 to the raw mean instead roughly doubles the positive rate (table above). Default used here: S2 (`s_score`) < -2.3.

- Fraction S2 < -2.3: single-orientation pairs 1.79%, reciprocal pairs 3.58% (reciprocal pairs are enriched for query genes, i.e. genes chosen as queries, often hubs).
